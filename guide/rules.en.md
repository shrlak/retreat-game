# Fellowship of Light Rules

Some Tempters have slipped in among your retreat friends. Decide whom to trust as you set out on five Missions together in this hidden-role deduction game.

## Two sides

Workers of Light: there are more of them, but most don't know who the Tempters are.

Tempters: there are fewer of them, but most know exactly who their teammates are.

When the game starts, everyone secretly checks their own role card on their phone.

## How to win

The Workers of Light win by completing 3 Missions. If the Prophet is in the game, they must also survive the Spy's Prophet Hunt at the end.

The Tempters win if any one of these happens:

- 3 Missions fail
- A team is rejected 5 times in a row on one Mission
- After 3 wins for the Workers of Light, the Spy correctly names the Prophet

## How a round goes

1. **Propose**: This round's Leader picks exactly the required number of players for the Mission. They may include themselves.
2. **Discuss**: Talk freely about why this team, and who seems suspicious.
3. **Vote**: Everyone taps Approve or Reject at the same time. Who voted which way is shown to all.
4. **Result**: If more than half approve, the team sets out. On a tie or more rejects, the Leader role moves to the next player.
5. **Mission**: Only team members secretly play a Blessing or Hindrance card. The Workers of Light can only play Blessing.
6. **Reveal**: The cards are shuffled and only the number of Hindrances is shown. Nobody knows who played what. One Hindrance is enough to fail.

The next round starts with the Leader role passing one seat along.

## Good to know

- With 7 or more players, the fourth Mission only fails if 2 Hindrances are played.
- If the fifth proposal on one Mission is rejected too, the Tempters win immediately. So the fifth vote really matters.
- Once a Mission goes ahead, the rejection count starts again from 0.
- The Tempters may play either Blessing or Hindrance. Sometimes they play Blessing on purpose to earn trust.
- Everyone can see which roles are in the game. Only who holds each role is secret.

## The final twist: Prophet Hunt

Completing 3 Missions is not quite the end. The Tempters talk it over, and the Spy names the one person they think is the Prophet.

If they are right, the Tempters steal the win. If they are wrong, the Workers of Light win for good. That's why the Prophet must stay hidden to the very end.

## The Lamp (optional rule)

This rule is used only if the host turns it on. Recommended for 7 or more players.

The player to the right of the first Leader starts with the Lamp.

After the 2nd, 3rd and 4th Missions, the Lamp holder picks one player and secretly learns their side (Workers of Light or Tempters).

The player who was checked receives the Lamp next. Anyone who has already held it cannot be picked.

Everyone sees who checked whom, but only the holder sees the answer. Whether to share it, or to share it truthfully, is up to them.

## Expansion: Good Lancelot and Evil Lancelot

This expansion is used only if the host turns it on. The two Lancelots join the game, and the Evil Lancelot takes one of the Tempters seats.

A deck of 5 Crossroads cards (3 Stay, 2 Switch sides) is shuffled. At the start of the 3rd, 4th and 5th Missions, one card is shown to everyone.

When a 'Switch sides' card comes up, the two Lancelots swap sides. If another one comes up, they swap back.

Mission cards, the Lamp's answer and the final result all use each Lancelot's current side. A Lancelot currently with the Workers of Light can only play Blessing.

- Quiet Crossroads: the Evil Lancelot and the other Tempters don't know each other. The Prophet still sees the Evil Lancelot as one of the Tempters.
- Known Crossroads: the two Lancelots recognise each other, and the Evil Lancelot and the other Tempters also know each other.
- The Evil Lancelot never carries out the Prophet Hunt.

## The Scroll of the Word (expansion)

When proposing a team, the Leader also hands the Scroll of the Word to one team member. They can't give it to themselves.

After everyone on the team has played a card, the Scroll of the Word holder may flip one other team member's card to the opposite. They can also choose not to.

Only the holder learns what the flipped card originally was. Everyone sees whose card was flipped, and by whom.

## Alternate team sizes (optional)

If the host turns this on, the team sizes change for 5 and 7 player games.

5 players: 3 · 2 · 3 · 2 · 3

7 players: 2 · 3 · 4 · 3 · 4 (the fourth Mission still needs 2 Hindrances)

## Table talk

- You can say anything out loud. Suspicion, excuses and bluffing are all part of the game.
- The one thing you may not do is show your screen or look at someone else's.
- Cover your screen when you vote and when you play a Mission card.
- Lying in the game is just play. When it's over, smile and bless one another.

## Roles

### Prophet · Workers of Light

_Knows everything, but can't say it_

**What you know**
- You can see most of the Tempters.
- But the Wolf in Sheep's Clothing is hidden from you.
- The Wanderer shows up as one of the Tempters.
- You don't know which Tempters role each of them has.

**Your goal**
- Guide the Workers of Light to complete 3 Missions.
- Keep the Spy from finding out who you are, right to the end.

**Tips**
- Saying "That person is one of the Tempters!" makes you an instant target. Nudge people with questions and votes instead.
- Now and then, act unsure or suspect the wrong person. Being too accurate gets you noticed.
- Reject teams that include the Tempters, but don't always be the only one who does.
- If the Watchman is in the game, they'll try to protect you. Trust them.

**Who can see you**: The Watchman sees you as a Prophet candidate.

### Watchman · Workers of Light

_The one who protects the Prophet_

**What you know**
- You can see Prophet candidates.
- If the False Prophet is in the game, you see two candidates and can't tell which one is real.
- If there is no False Prophet, the person you see is the real Prophet.

**Your goal**
- Work out the real Prophet and follow their hints.
- Keep the Spy from finding the Prophet.

**Tips**
- Compare which teams the two candidates approve and reject. Whoever matches the results better is more likely the real one.
- Acting as if you know things can draw the Spy's attention to you instead.
- Even when you're sure, never point out the real one in public. That puts the Prophet in danger.

**Who can see you**: Nobody sees you in any special way.

### Worker of Light · Workers of Light

_Fights with faith and sharp eyes_

**What you know**
- You have no information. That leaves you free to reason most honestly of all.

**Your goal**
- Uncover the Tempters and complete 3 Missions.

**Tips**
- Watch the vote history. Someone who keeps approving teams that fail is suspicious.
- Remember who was on teams that got a Hindrance. That narrows things down.
- Ask questions whenever you're curious. How people answer tells you a lot.
- At the end, acting as if you know something can confuse the Spy.

**Who can see you**: Nobody sees you in any special way.

### Spy · Tempters

_Holds the final move_

**What you know**
- You can see your fellow Tempters.
- But the Wanderer is hidden from you.

**Your goal**
- Make 3 Missions fail, or
- if the Workers of Light complete 3, find and name the Prophet at the end.

**Tips**
- All game long, watch who seems to know too much. Someone who avoids the Tempters from the very start may be the Prophet.
- Playing Hindrance every time gets you caught fast. Playing Blessing early can build trust.
- When you're on a team with a teammate, try to avoid both of you playing Hindrance.
- Before the final pick, talk it through with your teammates.

**Who can see you**: The Prophet sees you as one of the Tempters.

### False Prophet · Tempters

_Pretends to be a person of light_

**What you know**
- You can see your fellow Tempters.
- But the Wanderer is hidden from you.

**Your goal**
- Fool the Watchman into believing you are the Prophet.
- Make 3 Missions fail.

**Tips**
- Share opinions with confidence, like the real Prophet would. The Watchman is trying to decide between you and the real one.
- If you suspect who the real Prophet is, quietly let the Spy know.

**Who can see you**: The Watchman sees you as a Prophet candidate, and the Prophet sees you as one of the Tempters.

### Wolf in Sheep's Clothing · Tempters

_The hidden leader even the Prophet can't see_

**What you know**
- You can see your fellow Tempters.
- But the Wanderer is hidden from you.

**Your goal**
- Win the deep trust of the Workers of Light, and make 3 Missions fail.

**Tips**
- Even the Prophet doesn't know you. So act boldly, like one of the Workers of Light.
- If someone suspects you, it's only guesswork, not something the Prophet saw. Stay calm and explain yourself.
- Playing a Hindrance on an important Mission after you've earned trust hits hardest.

**Who can see you**: Even the Prophet can't see you. Only your fellow Tempters know you.

### Wanderer · Tempters

_One of the Tempters, wandering alone_

**What you know**
- You can't see anyone.
- The other Tempters don't know who you are either.

**Your goal**
- Make 3 Missions fail, even without knowing your teammates.

**Tips**
- Since you don't know your teammates, watch who was on teams that got a Hindrance.
- A teammate may be on your team. Two Hindrances puts suspicion on both of you, so pick your moment.
- Remember that the Prophet sees you as one of the Tempters.

**Who can see you**: The Prophet sees you as one of the Tempters. Your fellow Tempters can't see you.

### Tempter · Tempters

_Quietly stirs things up_

**What you know**
- You can see your fellow Tempters.
- But the Wanderer is hidden from you.

**Your goal**
- Make 3 Missions fail, and help find the Prophet.

**Tips**
- Suspect people and ask questions like one of the Workers of Light. Staying too quiet stands out.
- Don't defend a suspected teammate too hard. You may get caught together.
- Remember who looks like the Prophet, and tell your teammates at the final pick.

**Who can see you**: The Prophet sees you as one of the Tempters.

### Good Lancelot · Workers of Light

_A Lancelot of the light, standing at a crossroads_

**What you know**
- Quiet Crossroads: you can't see anyone.
- Known Crossroads: you can see the Evil Lancelot, and they know you too.

**Your goal**
- Help your current side win. You start with the Workers of Light.
- From the 3rd Mission on, a 'Switch sides' card can move you to the Tempters.

**Tips**
- Every time a Crossroads card is turned over, check your current side again. The result uses your side at the very end.
- While you're with the Workers of Light, you can only play Blessing. With the Tempters, you can play Hindrance too.
- Your side may change, so don't sound too certain too early.

**Who can see you**: In Known Crossroads, the Evil Lancelot can see you. Otherwise nobody can.

### Evil Lancelot · Tempters

_A Lancelot in shadow, standing at a crossroads_

**What you know**
- Quiet Crossroads: you can't see anyone, and the other Tempters don't know you.
- Known Crossroads: you can see your fellow Tempters and the Good Lancelot, and they know you too.

**Your goal**
- Help your current side win. You start with the Tempters.
- From the 3rd Mission on, a 'Switch sides' card can move you to the Workers of Light.

**Tips**
- The Prophet sees you as one of the Tempters from the start, and isn't told if your side changes.
- If you end up with the Workers of Light, you can only play Blessing.
- You never carry out the Prophet Hunt. Just share your opinion with your teammates.

**Who can see you**: The Prophet sees you as one of the Tempters. In Known Crossroads, your fellow Tempters and the Good Lancelot see you too.

## Glossary

- **Workers of Light**: The side of light. More players, but less information.
- **Tempters**: The hidden side. Fewer players, but they know each other.
- **Mission**: One of 5 tasks. The first side to take 3 wins.
- **Leader**: The player picking this Mission team. Passes to the next seat after each proposal.
- **Blessing / Hindrance**: The cards team members secretly play. The Workers of Light can only play Blessing.
- **Approve / Reject**: The vote on whether to send the Leader's team.
- **Lamp**: Optional rule. A lamp that secretly reveals one player's side.
- **Scroll of the Word**: Expansion. Lets one team member flip another member's card.
- **Crossroads card**: Expansion. Revealed one at a time from the 3rd Mission, and can switch the Lancelots' sides.
- **Prophet Hunt**: After 3 wins for the Workers of Light, the Spy's last chance to find and name the Prophet.
