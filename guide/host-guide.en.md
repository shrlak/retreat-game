# Fellowship of Light Host Guide

A guide to help you run a fun game in about 30 minutes at a retreat or small group, even with first-time players.

## What you need

- One phone per person (Wi-Fi or mobile data)
- 5 to 10 players per room. With more people, split into several rooms. 7 or 8 is the sweet spot.
- Seats in a circle. Being able to see each other's faces matters.
- A game takes 20 to 40 minutes. Allow 40 minutes for the first one, including the explanation.

## Before you start (5 minutes)

1. The host creates a room and reads out or shows the room code.
2. Once everyone is in, sit in a circle in the order shown on screen. The Group Leader role goes around in this order.
3. Turn on roles to fit your group size. See the recommended setups below.
4. Read the '3-minute explanation' below, and start once everyone taps Ready.

## Recommended setups

- First game: turn on only the Prophet and the Spy. Every other seat is filled automatically as a Worker of Light or a Tempter.
- Once you're comfortable: add the Watchman and the False Prophet. They keep each other in check and add a lot of fun.
- 7 or more players: add the Wolf in Sheep's Clothing and turn on the Lamp.
- 10 players: add the Wanderer too, so all 4 of the Tempters have special roles.

## Expansions to try later

- Good Lancelot and Evil Lancelot: from the 3rd Mission, Crossroads cards are revealed and the two Lancelots may swap sides. For a first try, use 'Quiet Crossroads'.
- Scroll of the Word: the Group Leader hands it to a team member, who may flip one other member's card. Great with 7 or more.
- Alternate team sizes: changes the team sizes in 5 and 7 player games. Nice when the same group plays several rounds.
- Add only one expansion at a time. Long explanations are hard on first-time players.

## 3-minute explanation

> "Some Tempters are secretly hiding among us. Everyone else is one of the Workers of Light."
>
> "There are 5 Missions, and the first side to take 3 wins."
>
> "Each time, the Group Leader picks a team and we all vote Approve or Reject."
>
> "An approved team secretly plays Blessing or Hindrance cards. One Hindrance and the Mission fails. The Workers of Light can only play Blessing."
>
> "The Prophet knows who the Tempters are but must not get caught. Even if the Workers of Light win, the Spy can turn it around by naming the Prophet."
>
> "You can say anything, but never show your phone screen. Ready to check your cards?"

## While you play

- If discussion drags on, set a limit of about 2 to 3 minutes per proposal.
- Asking quiet players "What do you think?" helps everyone join in.
- If suspicion piles onto one person, especially a newcomer or a younger player, lighten the mood.
- Agree beforehand not to use faith statements like "I swear to God" as proof in the game. A game is a game, and faith is faith.
- When the fifth proposal comes around, remind everyone: "If this one is rejected, the Tempters win!"

## If something goes wrong

- A phone turned off or the app closed: reopening on the same phone returns the player to their seat and role.
- Someone needs a different phone: after they've been disconnected for 15 seconds, the Host taps 'Allow rejoin'. For 2 minutes, the new phone can join with the same name.
- The Host's phone went off: after a minute, a 'Take over as host' button appears for the others. Whoever taps it takes over.
- One player is stuck on the card reveal: the host can tap Skip to start the game.
- Someone accidentally showed their screen: treat this game as practice, play it out, then open a new game with Restart when it ends.
- Rules feel confusing: the guide button is available any time, even during a game.

## After the game (5-minute sharing)

- Every role is revealed. Talk about who noticed what, and when.
- Laugh it off with questions like "Why did you reject that one?"
- Say "Sorry, and well played" to anyone you suspected.

### Optional sharing questions

- Whose words did you trust today, and why?
- What helped you tell the true from the false? (See Matthew 10:16 and 1 John 4:1.)
- What does it mean to trust one another in a community?

## One-page summary (1-Minute Guide)

### What is this game?

- Some Tempters are secretly hiding among us.
- The Workers of Light win by completing 3 of 5 Missions with Blessings. The Tempters win by spoiling 3 Missions with Hindrances.
- Talk, vote and figure out who is who. For 5 to 10 players.

### How a round works

1. The Group Leader picks a Mission team.
2. Everyone votes Approve or Reject at the same time. More than half must approve.
3. If approved, only team members secretly play a Blessing or Hindrance card.
4. Even one Hindrance card means the Mission fails.
5. If rejected, the next player becomes Group Leader. Five rejections in a row on one Mission and the Tempters win at once.

### The roles in one line

- **Prophet**: Knows the Tempters from the start. Helps quietly without getting caught.
- **Watchman**: Sees two Prophet candidates. Protect the real one.
- **Worker of Light**: Knows nothing. Wins with sharp eyes and good reasoning.
- **Spy**: If the Workers of Light win, gets one last chance to find the Prophet and steal the win.
- **False Prophet**: Looks like the Prophet to the Watchman. Pretends to be the real one.
- **Wolf in Sheep's Clothing**: The hidden leader that even the Prophet cannot see.
- **Wanderer**: One of the Tempters, but doesn't know the others, and they don't know them.
- **Tempter**: An ordinary member of the Tempters. Knows their teammates.
- **Good Lancelot**: (Expansion) Starts with the Workers of Light, but a Crossroads card can switch their side.
- **Evil Lancelot**: (Expansion) Starts with the Tempters, but a Crossroads card can switch their side.

### Remember

- Never show your phone screen to anyone.
- You can say anything out loud. Lying in the game is just part of the fun.
- The Workers of Light can only play Blessing.
- Even after 3 wins for the Workers of Light, the Tempters win if the Spy finds the Prophet.
